# Scheduling, closing and limiting responses

Go to **Form settings > Availability** to control when a form accepts responses.

**Open and close dates**
Set an opening and/or closing date and time. Times use the time zone set in Settings > Account. Scheduling is available on all plans.

**Response cap**
Close a form automatically after a set number of responses — for example, 50 event registrations. Available on all plans. This cap is separate from your account's monthly response limit (KB-025).

**Closed message**
Free forms show a standard "This form is closed" message. On Starter and above you can write a custom closed message.

**Closing manually**
Use the **Accepting responses** toggle at the top of the form to close or reopen it at any time. Closed forms keep all their responses, and reopening uses the same link.

**One response per person**
- **Browser limit (all plans):** turn on "One response per person" to stop repeat submissions from the same browser using a cookie. Respondents can get around it with another browser or device.
- **Email verification (Pro and above):** respondents confirm their email address with a code, and each address can respond only once.
