# Upgrading and downgrading your plan

Only the account Owner can change the plan, from Settings > Billing > Change plan.

**Upgrades** take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period, not the full monthly price, and the new limits (responses, storage, seats) apply right away.

**Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep your current plan's features until then. Downgrades do not generate partial refunds (KB-002).

**If you exceed the new plan's limits**
- **Forms:** On Free, only the 3 most recently edited forms stay active. Other forms are deactivated, not deleted, and their responses remain viewable and exportable. Upgrade again to reactivate them.
- **Seats:** Remove team members down to the new plan's seat count before the downgrade can be scheduled (KB-014).
- **Features:** Features not in the new plan are paused — for example, custom domains and Stripe payment fields when leaving Pro, or SSO when leaving Business. Integrations that need a higher plan are paused, not deleted (KB-027).
- **Responses:** The new monthly response limit applies from the first billing period on the new plan.

You can cancel a scheduled downgrade any time before it takes effect.
