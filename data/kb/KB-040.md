# Viewing results and sharing reports

**Results tab (all plans)**
Open a form and click **Results** to see:
- **Summary:** charts for every question, updated as responses arrive.
- **Responses:** each individual response in a table or one at a time.

**Filtering**
Filter by date range or by answers on all plans. Saving filters for reuse is available on Starter and above.

**Sharing a report**
On **Pro and Business**, click **Share report** to create a read-only link to the summary. You can protect it with a password and turn the link off at any time. People with the link do not need a Larkspur account or seat. On Free and Starter, share results by exporting them (KB-026).

**Editing and deleting responses**
Owners, Admins and Editors can edit or delete responses; Viewers can only view. Deleted responses move to the form's **Trash** and can be restored for **30 days**, after which they are permanently deleted.

**Notes and tags (Pro and Business)**
Add internal notes and tags to responses to track follow-up.

Edits to responses are not sent to Google Sheets (KB-030).
