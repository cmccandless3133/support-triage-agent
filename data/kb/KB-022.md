# Troubleshooting forms that won't load or display

**1. Check for an incident**
Look at status.larkspur-forms.example (KB-018).

**2. Check the form's state**
A form that shows a message instead of its fields may be:
- **Closed** by a close date or response cap (KB-038).
- **Not accepting responses** because the account reached its monthly response limit (KB-025).
- **Deactivated** because the account moved to Free and the form is beyond the 3-active-form limit (KB-004).

**3. Embedded form shows a blank space**
- Your website's Content Security Policy must allow `https://*.larkspur-forms.example` in `frame-src` and `script-src`. A browser console message such as "Refused to frame" means the policy is blocking the form; your web developer needs to add the domain.
- Some website builders strip `<script>` tags. Use the plain iframe embed instead.
- Ad blockers and content blockers can hide embeds, including on phones; test in a private window.
- Corporate firewalls must allow `*.larkspur-forms.example`.

**4. Embedded form is cut off**
Plain iframe embeds have a fixed height. Increase the iframe's height value, or use the JavaScript inline embed, which resizes automatically (KB-035).

**5. Browser issues**
Confirm the browser is supported and JavaScript is enabled (KB-019).
