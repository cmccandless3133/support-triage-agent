# Invoices, receipts and VAT

**Finding invoices**
An invoice is emailed to the billing contact after every successful charge. All past invoices are available as PDFs in Settings > Billing > Invoices. The Owner and Admins can download them.

**Changing invoice details**
Update the company name, billing address, VAT ID and billing contact email in Settings > Billing > Billing details. Changes apply to future invoices.

**Reissuing invoices**
Invoices issued in the **last 60 days** can be reissued showing an updated company name, address or VAT ID — contact support with the invoice numbers. The tax amount on a reissued invoice does not change. Invoices older than 60 days cannot be changed.

**VAT, GST and sales tax**
Tax is calculated from your billing address. Business customers in the EU and UK can enter a VAT ID in Billing details; once it is validated, future invoices use the reverse-charge mechanism (0% VAT). A VAT ID applies to future charges only — VAT already charged is not refunded.

**Bank transfer and purchase orders**
Available only on **Business annual** plans. All other plans are paid by card (KB-009).

Invoice numbers use the format LF-YYYY-NNNNNN.
