# Supported browsers

**Form builder and dashboard**
- The latest two major versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.
- Internet Explorer is not supported, and the builder will not load in it.
- The builder needs a screen at least 1024 pixels wide. Building forms on phones is not supported, but you can view responses in a mobile browser.

**Published forms (respondents)**
- The latest two major versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on desktop.
- Safari on iOS 15 or later and Chrome on Android 10 or later.
- JavaScript must be enabled. Forms do not load with JavaScript turned off.

**Troubleshooting display problems**
1. Update the browser to a supported version.
2. Clear the cache and reload the page.
3. Try a private/incognito window to rule out extensions; ad blockers and content blockers can hide embedded forms.
4. Check status.larkspur-forms.example for incidents.

If a published form is blank only when embedded on your website, see KB-022. If it shows "This form is not accepting responses", see KB-025 and KB-038.

When contacting support about a browser problem, include the browser name and version, the device, and a screenshot of any error.
