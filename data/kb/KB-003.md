# Annual billing and switching billing cycles

Annual plans cost the same as 10 months of monthly billing, so you get 2 months free:

- Starter: $190/year (instead of $228)
- Pro: $490/year (instead of $588)
- Business: $1,290/year (instead of $1,548)

**Switching from monthly to annual**
Go to Settings > Billing > Billing cycle and choose Annual. The change takes effect immediately: you are charged the annual price, and the unused part of your current month is credited against it, so you don't lose what you already paid. Only the account Owner can change the billing cycle.

**Switching from annual to monthly**
You can schedule the switch at any time, but it takes effect at the end of your current annual term. An annual term cannot be converted to monthly part-way through.

**Renewals**
Annual plans renew automatically. We email the billing contact a renewal reminder **14 days before** the renewal date. To stop renewal, turn off Auto-renew in Settings > Billing before the renewal date; the plan stays active until the end of the term and then moves to Free.

Response limits on annual plans still reset every month (KB-025). Refunds for annual plans follow KB-002.
