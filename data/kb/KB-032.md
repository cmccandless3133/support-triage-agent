# Mailchimp integration

Available on **Starter and above**; it is not available on Free. The integration adds respondents to a Mailchimp audience.

**Connecting**
1. Open the form and go to Integrations > Mailchimp > Connect.
2. Sign in to Mailchimp and choose one audience. Each form connection sends to one audience.
3. Map the form's **Email** field (required). First name, last name and tags are optional.

**Consent**
Add a consent checkbox to your form and set the integration to add contacts **only when the checkbox is ticked**. You can also turn on **double opt-in**, so Mailchimp emails each new contact to confirm.

**Existing contacts**
If an email address is already in the audience, the contact is updated, not duplicated.

**Common errors** (see Form > Integrations > Activity)
- *"Member in compliance state"*: the person previously unsubscribed. Mailchimp does not allow unsubscribed contacts to be re-added through an integration; they must re-subscribe through a Mailchimp signup form.
- *"Invalid resource"*: the email field was empty or not a valid address.
- *"Audience not found"*: the audience was deleted; reconnect and choose a new one.

If the account moves below Starter, the integration is paused (KB-027).
