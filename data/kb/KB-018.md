# System status, uptime and SLA

**Status page**
Live service status and incident history are published at status.larkspur-forms.example. Subscribe there for email updates when an incident starts and is resolved.

**Uptime target**
We target 99.9% monthly uptime on every plan. A contractual **uptime SLA is offered only on the Business plan**.

**Business SLA credits**
- Monthly uptime below 99.9%: credit of **10%** of that month's fee.
- Monthly uptime below 99.0%: credit of **25%** of that month's fee.
- Request the credit from support within **30 days** of the end of the incident.
- Credits are applied to future invoices and are not paid as cash refunds.
- Scheduled maintenance and problems caused by your own systems (for example, your webhook endpoint) are excluded.

Free, Starter and Pro plans do not receive service credits.

**Scheduled maintenance**
Announced on the status page at least **72 hours** in advance, usually Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Published forms keep accepting responses during most maintenance windows.

**During an incident**
If a form cannot submit during an outage, the respondent sees an error and that response is not saved. Responses saved before an incident are not affected.
