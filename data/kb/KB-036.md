# Custom domains

Custom domains let your forms load at an address such as `forms.yourbusiness.example`.

**Availability**
- Pro: **1** custom domain
- Business: **5** custom domains
- Not available on Free or Starter

**Requirements**
You must use a **subdomain** (for example, `forms.yourbusiness.example`). Root/apex domains such as `yourbusiness.example` are not supported.

**Setup**
1. Go to Settings > Custom domains > Add domain and enter the subdomain.
2. At your DNS provider, create a **CNAME** record pointing the subdomain to `custom.larkspur-forms.example`.
3. If your DNS provider offers proxying for the record, turn it off.
4. Click **Verify**.

**Timing**
DNS changes can take up to **48 hours** to propagate. After the domain verifies, an SSL certificate is issued automatically within **24 hours**. Until it is issued, visitors may see a "not secure" or certificate warning; this is expected and needs no action.

**Downgrades**
If the account moves below Pro, forms revert to their standard larkspur-forms.example links. Links using the custom domain stop working until you upgrade.

**Changing domains**
Removing a domain frees the slot immediately; old links on that domain stop working.
