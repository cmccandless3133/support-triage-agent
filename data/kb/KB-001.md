# Plans and pricing overview

Larkspur Forms has four plans. Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes.

- **Free** — $0. 3 active forms, 100 responses/month, 10 MB max file upload, 100 MB storage, 1 seat. Forms show the "Made with Larkspur" badge.
- **Starter** — $19/month or $190/year. Unlimited forms, 1,000 responses/month, 25 MB max file upload, 2 GB storage, 1 seat. Adds badge removal, conditional logic, and the Slack, Zapier and Mailchimp integrations.
- **Pro** — $49/month or $490/year. Unlimited forms, 10,000 responses/month, 100 MB max file upload, 20 GB storage, 3 seats. Adds Stripe payment collection, custom domains, webhooks and the REST API.
- **Business** — $129/month or $1,290/year. Unlimited forms, 50,000 responses/month, 250 MB max file upload, 100 GB storage, 10 seats. Adds SAML single sign-on, audit log, a 99.9% uptime SLA and priority support.

The Google Sheets integration is included on every plan, including Free.

**Annual billing** charges 10 months' price for 12 months of service (2 months free). See KB-003.

**Support response targets:** Free — 2 business days by email; Starter and Pro — 1 business day; Business — 4 business hours. Live chat is available to Pro and Business customers Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 US Eastern.

Limits on responses, uploads and seats are described in KB-025, KB-020 and KB-014.
