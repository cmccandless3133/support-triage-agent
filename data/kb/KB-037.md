# Templates, duplicating and transferring forms

**Templates**
More than 300 templates are available on every plan from **Create form > Templates**, including event registration, restaurant reservation, feedback and order forms. Templates that use paid features, such as payments or logic, are labelled with the plan they need.

On Pro and Business you can save any form as a **team template** that teammates can reuse.

**Duplicating a form**
Open the form menu (⋯) and choose **Duplicate**. The copy includes fields, logic, theme and settings. It does **not** include responses or integrations — reconnect integrations on the copy. A duplicate counts toward the Free plan's limit of 3 active forms.

**Transferring a form to another Larkspur account**
Available on **Starter and above**.
1. Open the form menu and choose **Transfer**.
2. Enter the email address of the receiving account's Owner.
3. The recipient has **7 days** to accept.

Transferred forms move with all their responses and uploaded files. Integrations are disconnected and must be reconnected by the new owner. The receiving account must have room under its own plan limits.

**Within a team**
Forms belong to the account, not the person who created them, so no transfer is needed between team members.
