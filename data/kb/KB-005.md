# Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, the account enters a 7-day retry period.

**Retry schedule**
We retry the card **3 times over 7 days**: on day 1, day 3 and day 7 after the failed charge. The Owner and billing contact receive an email after each failed attempt. You keep full access to your plan during the retry period.

**Fixing it**
Update your card in Settings > Billing > Payment method. As soon as a new card is saved, we retry the outstanding invoice immediately.

**If all retries fail**
After the third failed retry, the account is automatically **downgraded to Free**. Nothing is deleted: forms, responses and files are kept, but forms beyond the Free limit of 3 active forms are deactivated and paid features are paused (KB-004). You can resubscribe at any time from Settings > Billing, and deactivated forms are reactivated as soon as your plan allows them.

**Common causes**
- Expired or replaced card
- Insufficient funds
- Your bank blocking an online or international charge — charges appear on statements as "LARKSPUR FORMS"
- A 3-D Secure confirmation that was not completed

Larkspur does not charge late fees for failed payments.
