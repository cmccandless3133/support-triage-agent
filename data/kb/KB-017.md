# Privacy and data subject requests (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA)

Larkspur is the **data processor** for responses collected through customers' forms, and the **data controller** for account holder information such as names, emails and billing details.

**All data subject requests go to the privacy team**
Requests to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or similar laws are handled only by the privacy team at privacy@larkspur-forms.example. Support cannot fulfil, confirm or refuse these requests and does not search for a person's data in a ticket. Support forwards every such request to the privacy team.

The privacy team responds within **30 days** and may need to verify the requester's identity.

**Respondents**
If you submitted a form run by a Larkspur customer, that customer controls your response data. The privacy team will coordinate your request with the form owner.

**Account holders**
You can export your own data at any time (KB-026) and delete your account (KB-016). Formal legal requests are still handled by the privacy team.

**Data Processing Agreement**
Customers on paid plans can sign our standard DPA in Settings > Legal. Custom DPA terms are not offered.
