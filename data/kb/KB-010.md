# Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your account email address.
3. Open the email from no-reply@larkspur-forms.example and click the reset link.

**Important details**
- Reset links are valid for **60 minutes** and can be used only once. If a link has expired, request a new one; an expired link does not mean anything is wrong with the account.
- You can request up to **5 reset emails per hour**. Further requests in that hour are not sent, so wait an hour before trying again.
- If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check spam or junk folders and confirm you are using the address the account was registered with.
- New passwords must be at least **10 characters** and cannot match any of your last 5 passwords.
- Resetting your password signs you out of all other sessions and disconnects the Zapier integration, which must be reconnected (KB-028).

**Accounts using single sign-on**
If your organization enforces SSO (Business plan), reset your password with your identity provider instead.

**What support can and cannot do**
Support can confirm whether a reset email was sent, but can never set, view or tell you a password. If you no longer have access to the email address on the account, see KB-012.
