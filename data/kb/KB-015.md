# Locked accounts and suspicious activity

**Account lockouts**
After **10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts**, the account is locked for **30 minutes**. We also email an unlock link to the account address; clicking it unlocks the account immediately, so you don't need to wait for support. If you're unsure of your password, reset it (KB-010) after unlocking.

**Reviewing activity**
Settings > Security > Sessions shows sign-ins from the last **90 days**, with device, IP address and approximate location. **Sign out all sessions** ends every session except your current one.

**If you see activity you don't recognize**
1. Reset your password immediately.
2. Sign out all sessions.
3. Turn on 2FA (KB-011).
4. Report it to security@larkspur-forms.example.

Support escalates every report of suspected unauthorized access or data exposure to the security team, which investigates what was accessed. Do not include passwords in support tickets.

**We will never ask for your password or 2FA codes** by email, chat or phone.

**Phishing and abuse**
If a Larkspur form is being used for phishing or to collect passwords, use the **Report abuse** link at the bottom of the form or email abuse@larkspur-forms.example.
