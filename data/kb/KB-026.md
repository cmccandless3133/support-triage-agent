# Exporting your data

Exports are available on every plan, including Free, and for deactivated forms.

**Exporting responses from one form**
Open the form and go to Results > Export. Choose CSV, XLSX (Excel) or JSON. Each export file contains up to **50,000 responses**; larger exports are split into multiple files automatically. You can filter by date range before exporting.

**Exporting uploaded files**
Results > Export > Uploaded files downloads all files from the form as ZIP archives of up to **5 GB** each.

**Exporting your whole account**
The Owner or an Admin can go to Settings > Data > Export everything. The export includes form definitions (JSON), all responses and all uploaded files. Generating it can take up to **24 hours**. We email a download link that is valid for **7 days**.

**Forms in Trash**
Deleted forms can be restored from Trash within 30 days and then exported.

**Ongoing copies**
To keep a live copy of responses outside Larkspur, use the Google Sheets integration (KB-030), available on all plans.

If you plan to delete your account, run the full export first. After the 30-day grace period, data cannot be recovered (KB-016).
