# Themes, logos and branding

**Themes (all plans)**
Open the form and click **Design** to change fonts, colors, button styles and background images. You can save a theme and apply it to other forms.

**Logo (all plans)**
Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG logo up to **2 MB** in Design > Logo.

**Removing the "Made with Larkspur" badge**
The badge is removed automatically from all forms on **Starter and above**; there is no setting to change. It cannot be removed on Free. If you have just upgraded and still see the badge, reload the form without cache.

**Custom CSS (Pro and above)**
Pro and Business can add custom CSS in Design > Advanced. Custom CSS is not available on Free or Starter. Support can help with Larkspur's theme settings but does not write or debug custom CSS.

**Brand kits (Business)**
Business accounts can create shared brand kits with fixed colors, fonts and logos that every team member uses.

**Thank-you and closed screens**
Thank-you screens can be edited on all plans. Custom closed messages require Starter or above (KB-038).

**Tips**
- Keep enough contrast between text and background colors so forms stay readable.
- Preview on mobile before publishing; background images are cropped on small screens.
