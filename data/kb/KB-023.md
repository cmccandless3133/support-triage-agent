# Email notifications not arriving

**How notifications work**
New-response notifications are sent from notifications@larkspur-forms.example, usually within **2 minutes** of a submission. Configure them in Form settings > Notifications.

- Free: notifications go only to the account Owner's email.
- Starter and above: up to **10 recipients** per form.
- Responses flagged as spam do not trigger notifications (KB-021).

**If notifications stop arriving**
1. Check spam/junk folders and add notifications@larkspur-forms.example to your safe senders list.
2. Confirm the recipient addresses in Form settings > Notifications.
3. Look for a **suppressed address** banner. If an email to an address bounces, that address is suppressed for **7 days**. You can clear the suppression early from the notification settings once the mailbox is fixed.
4. Ask your IT team whether a company mail filter is quarantining automated messages.
5. Check Form settings > Notifications > Delivery log, which shows the last 14 days of notifications.

**Respondent confirmation emails**
Autoresponders that email respondents a copy of their answers are available on Starter and above.

**Sending from your own domain**
Pro and Business can send notifications and autoresponders from their own domain after adding the SPF and DKIM records shown in Settings > Email domain.
