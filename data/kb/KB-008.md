# Price changes

We occasionally change plan prices. When we do:

- We email the account Owner and billing contact at least **30 days before** a price change affects them.
- **Monthly plans:** the new price applies from the first renewal after the 30-day notice period ends.
- **Annual plans:** you keep your current price until the end of your current annual term. The new price applies at your next renewal. Additional years cannot be prepaid to lock in the old price.
- Discounts you already have, such as the 30% nonprofit discount (KB-007), continue to apply to the new price.

**Legacy plans**
If a plan you are on is retired, you can keep it at its current price for as long as the subscription stays active and unchanged. If you switch plan or billing cycle, or the subscription is cancelled or downgraded after failed payments, you move to current pricing and cannot return to the legacy plan.

**Avoiding the change**
You can downgrade or cancel before the new price takes effect (KB-004 and KB-009). A price change is not grounds for a refund outside the refund policy (KB-002).
