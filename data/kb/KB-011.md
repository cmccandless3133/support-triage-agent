# Two-factor authentication (2FA): setup and recovery

**Setting up 2FA**
Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. Larkspur supports authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported.

When you turn on 2FA you receive **10 one-time backup codes**. Each code works once. You can generate a new set at any time, which invalidates the old ones.

**Lost your device?**
Sign in with a backup code, then go to Settings > Security to remove the old device and add the new one.

**Lost your device and your backup codes**
2FA can only be removed after **account-owner verification**:
1. Email support **from the email address registered on the account**.
2. Provide your most recent invoice number (paid plans) or the names of two forms in the account (Free), plus the approximate date the account was created.
3. The security team reviews the request within **3 business days** and removes 2FA only if verification succeeds.

**Support can never disable 2FA over live chat or phone**, or for any request sent from an email address other than the registered one. There are no exceptions, including urgent requests.

**Teams**
On Pro and Business, the Owner can require 2FA for all members. Admins cannot remove another member's 2FA; that member must follow the recovery steps above.
