# Google Sheets integration

Available on **all plans, including Free**.

**Connecting**
1. Open the form and go to Integrations > Google Sheets > Connect.
2. Sign in with your Google account and allow access.
3. Create a new spreadsheet or choose an existing one. Larkspur adds a header row with your field names.

New responses are added as new rows, usually within **1 minute**.

**Keep the sync working**
- Don't rename the sheet tab Larkspur writes to.
- Don't rename, reorder or delete the header row, and don't move or insert columns between mapped columns. Add your own columns to the right of the last mapped column.
- If Larkspur's access is revoked in the Google account's security settings, the integration disconnects and must be reconnected.

**What does not sync**
Edits to existing responses and deleted responses are not reflected in the sheet.

**Fixing a broken sync**
Undo the changes to the header row or tab name, or reconnect the integration. Then use **Resync** in the integration settings to resend missing responses. Resync sends up to the most recent **10,000 responses**.

**Sheet size**
Google Sheets allows 10 million cells per spreadsheet. When that is reached, syncing stops; connect a new spreadsheet to continue.
