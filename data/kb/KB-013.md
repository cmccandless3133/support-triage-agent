# Single sign-on (SAML SSO)

SAML single sign-on is available **only on the Business plan**. Starter and Pro accounts cannot use SAML SSO, though every plan can use "Sign in with Google" for individual logins.

**Supported identity providers**
Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, OneLogin, and any identity provider that supports SAML 2.0.

**Setup**
1. The Owner or an Admin opens Settings > Security > Single sign-on.
2. Verify your email domain by adding the DNS TXT record shown.
3. Enter your identity provider's metadata URL or upload its XML file.
4. Test the connection before enabling it for the team.

**Enforcing SSO**
When **Enforce SSO** is on, all members must sign in through your identity provider and password logins are disabled for everyone except the Owner, who keeps a password-based "break-glass" login. If your identity provider is unavailable, the Owner can sign in with the break-glass login and temporarily turn off enforcement; members can then use "Forgot password" (KB-010) to sign in with a password until SSO is restored.

**Provisioning**
Users signing in through SSO for the first time are created automatically with the Editor role and use a seat (KB-014). SCIM provisioning is not supported, so removing someone from your identity provider does not free their Larkspur seat — remove them in Settings > Team.
