# Spam protection and CAPTCHA

**Built-in protection (all plans)**
Every form includes invisible honeypot protection that blocks most bots automatically.

**Larkspur Shield CAPTCHA (all plans, including Free)**
Turn it on in Form settings > Security > Larkspur Shield. A challenge is shown only to traffic that looks suspicious, so most real respondents never see it. Third-party CAPTCHA services cannot be used with Larkspur forms.

**Rate limits**
By default, each form accepts up to **20 submissions per IP address per hour**. On Pro and Business you can set this anywhere from 1 to 200.

**Block lists (Pro and Business)**
Block specific email domains or IP addresses, up to **500 entries** per account, in Settings > Security > Block list.

**The Spam folder**
Responses flagged as spam go to the form's Spam folder. They:
- do **not** count toward your monthly response limit,
- do not trigger notifications or integrations,
- are deleted automatically after **30 days**.

If a real response is flagged, open it and click **Not spam**. It moves to your responses, counts toward your limit, and is sent to your integrations.

If spam continues after enabling Shield, lower the rate limit (Pro and Business) or add block list entries.
