# Data security and compliance

**Encryption**
All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later.

**Data residency**
You choose where your data is stored when you create the account: **US (Virginia)** or **EU (Frankfurt)**. The region cannot be changed later. To use a different region, create a new account in that region and transfer your forms to it (KB-037).

**Certifications**
Larkspur holds a SOC 2 Type II report. Business customers can request it under NDA.

**GDPR**
A standard Data Processing Agreement is available on paid plans (KB-017).

**HIPAA**
Larkspur Forms is **not HIPAA compliant** and does **not sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)** on any plan, including Business. Do not use Larkspur to collect protected health information (PHI). Support escalates questions about HIPAA or healthcare compliance to the compliance team and cannot make commitments about them.

**Payment data**
Card details entered in Stripe payment fields go directly to Stripe and never touch Larkspur servers (KB-031). Never ask respondents for card numbers in regular text fields.

**Reporting a security issue**
Email security@larkspur-forms.example. Vulnerability reports are acknowledged within 2 business days. Suspected data exposure reported to support is escalated to the security team (KB-015).
