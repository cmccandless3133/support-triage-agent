# Cancelling your subscription and payment methods

**How to cancel**
The account Owner can cancel at any time in Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. After you cancel:
- You keep paid features until the end of the current paid period.
- The account then moves to the Free plan automatically. Forms beyond the Free limit of 3 active forms are deactivated, not deleted, and their responses stay available (KB-004).
- No further charges are made.

Cancelling does **not** issue a refund automatically. Refund eligibility is described in KB-002.

Cancelling does not delete your account or data. To delete the account permanently, see KB-016.

**Reactivating**
Resubscribe at any time from Settings > Billing. Deactivated forms are reactivated when you choose a plan that allows them.

**Accepted payment methods**
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards, processed by Stripe.
- Bank transfer and purchase orders on Business annual plans only (KB-006).
- PayPal, checks and cryptocurrency are **not** accepted.

Larkspur never asks for card details by email or chat. Update cards only in Settings > Billing > Payment method.
