# Refund policy

Refund eligibility depends on your billing cycle. Support can process any refund that meets this policy but cannot approve exceptions.

**Monthly plans (Starter, Pro, Business)**
- Full refund if requested within **14 days of your first paid charge** on the account.
- Monthly renewal charges after the first payment are **not refundable**.

**Annual plans**
- **Prorated refund** for the unused days of the term if requested within **30 days of the annual charge**. This applies to first purchases and renewals.
- After 30 days, annual charges are not refundable.

**Not refundable**
- Any charge outside the windows above, including partial months, unused responses, unused seats, or time remaining after a downgrade or cancellation.
- Prorated upgrade charges, unless they fall inside the windows above.

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Requests outside this policy are reviewed by the billing team and cannot be promised by support.

**Chargebacks:** If you dispute a Larkspur charge with your bank, the account is placed on hold until the dispute is resolved. Please contact billing@larkspur-forms.example first.

Payments that *your respondents* make through your forms are refunded from your own Stripe dashboard, not by Larkspur (KB-031).
