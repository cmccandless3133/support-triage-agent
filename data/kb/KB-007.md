# Nonprofit discount

Registered nonprofit organizations get **30% off** any paid plan — Starter, Pro or Business — on monthly or annual billing. On annual billing the 30% is applied to the annual price, so it stacks with the 2 months free. For example, Starter annual becomes $133/year, Pro annual $343/year and Business annual $903/year.

**Eligibility**
- Registered charities and nonprofits (for example, US 501(c)(3) organizations or an equivalent national charity registration).
- Not eligible: individuals, political parties and campaigns, government agencies, and schools or universities. Larkspur does not offer an education or student discount.

**How to apply**
1. The Owner goes to Settings > Billing > Nonprofit discount.
2. Upload proof of registration, such as a determination letter or charity register entry.
3. Applications are reviewed within **5 business days**.

**Details**
- The discount starts from your next charge after approval. It is not applied retroactively to past invoices.
- It cannot be combined with other coupons or promotions.
- An approved discount carries over automatically when you switch between monthly and annual billing.
- Eligibility is re-verified every **12 months**; we email the Owner 30 days before re-verification is due.
