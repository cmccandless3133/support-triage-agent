# Deleting your account

Only the account **Owner** can delete an account, from Settings > Account > Delete account. Requests from other team members or third parties cannot be actioned.

**Before you delete**
- Cancel any active paid subscription first (KB-009). The delete button is unavailable while a subscription is active.
- Export anything you want to keep (KB-026).

**30-day grace period**
Deleting deactivates the account rather than erasing it immediately. Forms stop accepting responses and team members lose access. If you sign in within **30 days** of deleting, you can restore the account with all forms, responses and files intact.

After 30 days, the account and all forms, responses, uploaded files and team memberships are permanently deleted. Support cannot recover data after this point.

**Refunds**
Deleting an account does not trigger a refund. Any refund follows the refund policy (KB-002).

**Deleting individual forms**
Deleted forms move to Trash and can be restored for 30 days.

**Formal privacy requests**
If you want to exercise a legal right to erasure under GDPR or similar laws, or you are a respondent asking for your data to be removed, the request is handled by the privacy team (KB-017).
