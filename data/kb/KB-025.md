# Monthly response limits

Each plan includes a number of responses per month, counted across **all forms** in the account:

- Free: 100
- Starter: 1,000
- Pro: 10,000
- Business: 50,000

**When the count resets**
The count resets on your billing date each month. On Free, it resets each month on the day the account was created. Annual plans also reset monthly. Unused responses do not roll over.

**Alerts**
The Owner receives an email at **80%** and **100%** of the limit.

**What happens at 100%**
All forms in the account show "This form is not accepting responses right now" until the count resets or you upgrade. Responses already collected are never deleted.

**Upgrading**
Upgrading raises the limit immediately, and forms start accepting responses again right away (KB-004).

**Business overage**
Business accounts can turn on overage in Settings > Billing to keep collecting beyond 50,000 responses for **$10 per additional 1,000 responses**, billed at the end of the month. Overage is not available on other plans.

**What does not count**
Responses in the Spam folder (KB-021) and test submissions made in Preview mode do not count toward the limit.
