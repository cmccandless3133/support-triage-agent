# Changing your account email address

**If you can sign in**
1. Go to Settings > Profile > Email.
2. Enter the new address, your current password, and a 2FA code if 2FA is on.
3. Click the confirmation link sent to the new address. The link is valid for **24 hours**.

A notice is also sent to the old address. If you did not make the change, the link in that notice reverses it within **72 hours**. You do not need access to the old inbox to change your email while you are signed in.

**If you cannot sign in and no longer have access to the old email**
For security, support cannot change an account's email address on request and cannot send reset links to a different address. These cases require account-owner verification by the security team, following the same process as 2FA recovery (KB-011). Support escalates these requests; they are not handled in chat.

**Team members**
Each person changes their own email address. Owners and Admins cannot change another member's email; instead, remove the member and invite the new address (KB-014).

**Single sign-on users**
If SSO is enforced, your email address is controlled by your identity provider.
