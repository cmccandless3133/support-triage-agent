# Slack integration

Available on **Starter and above**. The Slack integration posts a message to a channel for each new response.

**Connecting**
1. Open the form and go to Integrations > Slack > Connect.
2. Sign in to Slack. You need permission to install apps in your Slack workspace; if you don't have it, ask a workspace admin.
3. Choose a channel.

Each form can have up to **3 Slack connections**, each posting to one channel.

**Private channels**
Private channels don't appear in the channel list until you invite the bot. Type `/invite @Larkspur Forms` in the private channel, then select it in the integration settings.

**Message contents**
Messages include the first **20 fields** of the response. Uploaded files appear as links that open only for people signed in to the Larkspur account with access to the form. Spam-flagged responses are not posted.

**If messages stop**
- The channel was **renamed, archived or deleted**: edit the connection and choose the channel again.
- A Slack workspace admin **removed the Larkspur app**: reconnect from the form's Integrations page.
- The account moved below Starter: the integration is paused (KB-027).

Check Form > Integrations > Activity for the exact error.
