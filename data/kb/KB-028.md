# Zapier integration

Available on **Starter and above**.

**Connecting**
1. In Zapier, create a Zap and search for "Larkspur Forms".
2. Choose the **New Response** trigger.
3. Sign in with your Larkspur account and authorize access.
4. Select the form and test the trigger with a real submission.

Zaps trigger instantly when a response is submitted. Test submissions made in Preview mode are not sent to Zapier.

**If a Zap stops triggering**
- **Password changed:** Changing or resetting your Larkspur password disconnects Zapier. Reconnect the Larkspur account in Zapier.
- **Plan changed:** If the account moved to Free, the integration is paused (KB-027).
- **Zap turned off:** Zapier can turn off Zaps after repeated errors. Check the Zap's history in Zapier.
- **Form changed:** If you add, rename or delete fields after building the Zap, open the Zap, refresh the sample data with a new real submission and remap the fields. New fields do not appear in Zapier until you do this.

**Limits**
Zapier task usage and Zapier plan limits are billed by Zapier, not Larkspur. Larkspur support can confirm whether responses were sent from Larkspur (Form > Integrations > Activity) but cannot edit your Zaps.
