# Collecting payments with Stripe

Payment collection is available on **Pro and Business** only.

**Setting up**
1. Add a **Payment** field to your form.
2. Click **Connect Stripe** and sign in to, or create, your own Stripe account.
3. Set a fixed amount or link the price to answers in the form.

**Fees**
Larkspur charges **no additional transaction fee**. Stripe's standard processing fees apply and are charged by Stripe.

**What's supported**
- One-time payments in the currencies your Stripe account supports.
- Recurring subscriptions are **not** supported.
- A **test mode** toggle in the Payment field settings uses your Stripe test keys.

**Card security**
Card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored by Larkspur.

**Refunds, payouts and disputes**
Money collected through your forms belongs to your Stripe account. To refund a respondent, issue the refund from your **Stripe dashboard**. Larkspur support cannot refund, view or move payments made to you through your forms. Payouts, disputes and Stripe account verification are handled between you and Stripe.

**Downgrades**
If your account moves below Pro, payment fields are disabled and forms that contain a Payment field stop accepting responses until you upgrade again or remove the Payment field from the form.
