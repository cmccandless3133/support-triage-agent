# Team seats, roles and ownership

**Seats included**
Free: 1 · Starter: 1 · Pro: 3 · Business: 10

**Extra seats**
- Pro: $15 per seat per month, up to a maximum of 10 seats in total.
- Business: $12 per seat per month, with no maximum.
- Extra seats are not available on Free or Starter; upgrade to Pro to add teammates.

**Roles** (every role, including the Owner, uses one seat)
- **Owner** — one per account. Manages billing, plan changes, cancellation and account deletion.
- **Admin** — manages members, forms, integrations and security settings; can download invoices but cannot change the plan or payment method.
- **Editor** — creates and edits forms and views responses.
- **Viewer** — views responses and reports; cannot edit forms.

**Inviting members**
Go to Settings > Team > Invite. Invitations expire after **7 days**. A pending invitation reserves a seat, and new invitations cannot be sent or resent while all seats are in use.

**Removing members**
Removing a member frees the seat immediately. Forms they created stay in the account.

**Transferring ownership**
Only the current Owner can transfer ownership, and only to an existing Admin, from Settings > Team > Transfer ownership. It requires the Owner's password and 2FA code. Support cannot transfer ownership on anyone's behalf.
